About Us

Goa Car Rental offers self-drive car rental options for visitors exploring Goa. With a range of vehicles, we aim to make your journey comfortable and convenient. Pickup and drop can be arranged across Goa; location-based charges are confirmed before booking.

Rental Terms

  • Standard 24-hour rental timing is 9:00 AM to 9:00 AM.
  • Pickup and drop are available across Goa, subject to location and distance. Any applicable charges will be confirmed before booking.
  • Security deposit is separate from the rental amount.
  • Car model, availability, price and booking are confirmed directly with Goa Car Rental.
  • Please carry valid driving licence and required identification documents.
